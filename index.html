Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can you attend a repair?

For standard repairs, we typically attend within 48 hours of booking. Emergency callouts are available same-day across Greater London, subject to availability.

Are your tradespeople insured?

Yes. Every Rocket Repairs tradesperson is fully insured with public liability cover. All our tradespeople are qualified and experienced professionals.

Do you charge for quotes?

No. All quotes are free and come with no obligation. For standard jobs we quote a fixed price before work begins — no surprises on your invoice.

What areas do you cover?

We cover all London boroughs and surrounding areas within the M25. For locations further afield, drop us a line at hello@rocketrepair.net and we'll let you know.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, debit and credit cards, and invoicing for commercial clients. Payment is due on completion unless otherwise agreed.

Do you offer guarantees on your work?

All repairs come with a 12-month workmanship guarantee. If something we fixed develops the same fault, we'll return and put it right at no extra cost.

By booking a repair through Rocket Repairs, you agree to the following terms. These terms apply to all services provided by Rocket Repairs London Ltd.

Bookings and Quotes

All quotes are valid for 30 days from issue. Fixed-price quotes cover the scope of work described — additional work discovered on site will be quoted separately before proceeding. We reserve the right to decline a booking at our discretion.

Pricing and Payment

Prices are quoted exclusive of VAT where applicable. Payment is due on completion of work unless a payment schedule has been agreed in writing. Late payments may incur a charge of 2% per month.

Cancellations

Bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before the scheduled appointment. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a £49 callout fee.

Guarantee

All workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months from completion. This guarantee covers defects in our work only and does not extend to pre-existing issues, normal wear, or materials supplied by the client.

Liability

Rocket Repairs is fully insured with comprehensive public liability cover. Our liability is limited to the cost of the work performed. We are not liable for consequential loss or damage beyond the scope of the repair.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2017–2025

Rocket Repairs London Ltd respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What We Collect

When you book a repair or contact us, we collect your name, address, email, and details of the work required. We do not collect data beyond what is necessary to deliver our services.

How We Use It

Your data is used to schedule and deliver repairs, send quotes and invoices, and communicate about your booking. We will not use your information for marketing without your explicit consent.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except where necessary to deliver our services (e.g., a subcontractor attending your property) or where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain booking and invoice records for six years in line with UK tax obligations. Contact details are removed on request, subject to legal retention requirements.
